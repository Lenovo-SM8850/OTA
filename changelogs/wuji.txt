This is Android 17 QPR0 with October 2026 security patches

Notes:
==============================
- LineageOS-based source.
- Always dirty flash at your own risk. If your device bootloops, revert to an older build that boots.
- Includes Dolby Atmos and Dolby Vision.

Device changes:
==============================
Touch: Fix controller restarts and missed taps after idling
Telephony: Fix incoming SMS not arriving after a reboot
Audio: Fix call recording and in-call music
OTA: Update stock firmware with the ROM while keeping the pinned bootloader
Game mode: Apply the gaming thermal profile
Performance: Allow memory bandwidth to rise under heavy load
Connectivity: Enable Qualcomm CNE network optimization support
Bluetooth: Enable LE Audio idle notifications during calls
Display: Center letterboxed apps vertically
Dolby Atmos: Enable processing by default unless you have saved a different preference
Dolby Vision: Show a status bar icon during Dolby Vision playback
Carrier profiles: Keep the loader off until enabled and reset its baseline on first enable
