This is Android 17 QPR0 with October 2026 security patches

Notes:
==============================
- LineageOS-based source.
- Always dirty flash at your own risk. If your device bootloops, revert to an older build that boots.
- Includes Dolby Atmos and Dolby Vision.

Device changes:
==============================
GameTuning: Add Game Tuning, an app for per-game CPU, GPU, frame rate, temperature and graphics tuning
Display: Change brightness at speeds tuned to the panel, with gradual automatic dimming
LegionHalo: Pick colors from a draggable grid and fit color dialogs in landscape
VideoMotion: List enabled apps first and keep search results above the keyboard
Home: Keep the dock steady after portrait Home gestures and use smaller workspace and dock icons
